What it shows.

Direction is close to random

With price-only features, next-day accuracy lands near 50% and below the always-up baseline for most tickers. That is the expected result for liquid large caps.

It trails on return

Sitting in cash on predicted down days misses too many up days. Buy and hold wins on compound growth.

It can cut drawdowns

For AAPL and MSFT the worst peak-to-trough loss is much smaller, so the defensible use is risk management, not prediction.

How to test without fooling yourself.

Only the past

Every feature on day t uses prices up to day t. A test changes future prices and fails if any past feature moves.

Retrain forward

The random forest is retrained every 250 trading days on strictly earlier data, then predicts the next year it has never seen.

Beat a baseline

Accuracy is compared with always predicting up, and returns with buy and hold, with trading costs charged.
