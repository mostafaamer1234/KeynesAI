The KeynesAI Signal

One model, any ticker, and the record to judge it by

Pick a ticker to get tomorrow's call.

How the call is made. A random forest reads 13 price features (moving-average ratios, share of up days, volatility, momentum, volume) and estimates the chance of a higher close the next trading day. It is retrained on every completed session up to the last close. The track record retrains once a year on strictly earlier data and charges 5 basis points per trade. The live version uses 60 trees and the last three years so it answers in seconds. The study in the repository uses 200 trees from 2000.

For learning about model evaluation only. Not investment advice. Prices from Yahoo Finance, which can be delayed.
